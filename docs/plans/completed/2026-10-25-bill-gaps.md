# Fail sync-bills on a gap in the bills

## Design

### Scope

- `raksa meters sync-bills` fails, and the daily workflow emails its owner, when the bills of a meter leave a gap.
  This catches a utility bill that is a scan without text, like the 2026-02 heat bill, and a bill that never reached
  Huoneistotieto.
- The check groups the bill readings by meter family: the label without the meter ID (`Kaukolämpö`,
  `Sähkö yhteinen`, `Vesi`, `Jätevesi`). A meter change then keeps one series.
- Two kinds of gap:
  - **Between bills:** a bill period starts more than one day after the latest end of the earlier bills of the family.
  - **Before the first bill:** EstateApp holds no reading of the family on the day before the first bill period starts.
- The check runs before the plan, so a dry run reports a gap too.

### Out of scope

- Reading text from PDF attachments.
- A gap after the latest bill. The next bill is not due yet.

### Decisions

- **Detect the gap, not the PDF.** A scan carries no text, so the importer cannot tell a utility bill from any other
  PDF. Rejected: fail on a PDF from a utility payee, because the 2026-02 heat PDF, already entered by hand, would then
  fail every run until it leaves the two-year window. Rejected: a log warning, because it sends no email.
- **Fail the run.** The workflow emails only on a failure. The message names the family and the missing dates, and
  says that a bill may be a scan to enter by hand.
- **Periods may overlap.** A balancing bill spans several estimates, so the check compares each start with the latest
  end so far.

### Reuse

- `label_to_key` and `get_consumptions` in `meters/estateapp.py`, and the bill readings that `sync-bills` already
  builds.

### Risks and rollout

- **A first bill that starts a new series fails the run.** A new utility, or a new family name, has no reading before
  it. Enter one reading by hand, or accept the email once.
- Rollout: merge. A dry run against the live services today finds no gap.

## Tasks

### Task 1: Gap check

- **Goal:** a missing month or quarter in a meter family's bills fails `sync-bills` with a clear message.
- **Acceptance criteria:**
  - A bill period that starts more than one day after the latest earlier end of its family fails the run, and the
    message names the family and the missing dates.
  - A family whose first bill has no EstateApp reading of the family on the day before fails the run.
  - A meter change inside a family is no gap, and an overlapping balancing period is no gap.
  - The check fails before any submit, also in a dry run.
  - A dry run against the live services finds no gap.
- **Tests:**
  - `test_sync_bills_fails_on_a_gap` fails if a missing month between two bills, or a missing reading before the
    first bill, passes, or if a meter change or an overlapping balancing period is reported as a gap.
- **Model:** Opus.
- **Constraints:** change no reading that `sync-bills` plans or submits.
